What is this website about ?

As students, we created this website to represent the department of computer sciences at Efrei for a web programming project. The purpose of this is to create a website on our own, as a web developer would, introducing a facet of programming we hadn't done so far in our curriculum. This website is to introduce people to the team of wonderfull teachers working hard to give amazing courses, show example of projects we have done, and highlight the quality of our learning environment.
We hope you enjoyed our project as much as we enjoyed creating it. It was one of the more creative projects EFREI has assigned us, and we couldn't be more grateful to express our creative minds, showing that coding isn't just technique, it's a creative outlet, and that coding can be fun and engaging !
