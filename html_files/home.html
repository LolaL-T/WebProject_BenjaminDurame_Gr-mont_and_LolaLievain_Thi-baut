Who we are

About us

We are the Computer Science Departement at Efrei ! We create all courses, accompany students in their learning, and concieve projects with real uses. Our team is made up of doctors specialized in computer sciences, to ensure students are in the best learning environment possible to learn and ask questions.

Our mission

Our goal is to help our students reach their learning goals and develop their skills to become ressourceful engineers. With the help of our expert teachers and ressources given by Efrei, such as the JetBrains tools and Github Education, our students are given all the tools they need to succeed.

Our values

We want our engineers to be the best they can be, which is why we teach them core values to help them navigate in the real world, such as :


  • Ethical Hacking courses
  • Green IT
  • Limitations of LLMs
  • and much more...
